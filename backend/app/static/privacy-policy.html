Privacy Policy

Roundtable AI
Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how Roundtable AI collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app. For privacy questions or requests, contact rifter.sahil@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account information: Your account identifier, email address, and name when available.
  • Documents and interview profile: Job descriptions and resumes you choose to submit are processed to prepare an interview profile. We retain the extracted interview profile rather than the original uploaded files.
  • Interview information: Your selected interview mode and skill focus, session records, conversation content, transcripts, and generated feedback and evaluation reports.
  • Microphone data: Audio is transmitted during live interviews to enable speech recognition and spoken responses. We do not intentionally store raw microphone audio in our backend.
  • Third-party API credentials: If you choose to use your own Agora or Groq credentials, we collect and securely store the credentials you provide so we can connect to those services on your behalf.
  • Service and diagnostic information: Request information, technical logs, and error details needed to operate, maintain, and protect the service.

How we use information

We use information to create interview profiles, provide live interview sessions, process conversations, generate feedback, manage accounts and user settings, support optional third-party integrations, maintain the service, and prevent misuse.

Third-party services

Roundtable AI uses third-party services to provide its features:

  • Firebase and Google Cloud: Account authentication and data storage.
  • Agora: Live voice communication and interview sessions.
  • Deepgram: Speech recognition during interviews.
  • MiniMax: Generation of spoken responses.
  • Groq: Processing document and conversation text for interview profiles, responses, and evaluation reports.

When you use these features, relevant information is transmitted to the corresponding providers for processing. If you connect your own API credentials, those credentials are used to authenticate requests to the respective services. Their processing, data retention, and any international transfers are also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data protection

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information. Third-party API credentials stored by Roundtable AI are encrypted. Access to account-related information is restricted to authorized service operations.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Information sharing

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the features you use. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the service, or to respond to valid legal requests.

Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide and protect the service. You can request deletion of your account and associated information through the Account section in the app or by following the instructions on our account deletion page.

We aim to complete account deletion within 48 hours under normal operating conditions. This includes account information, saved interview profiles, session records, transcripts and feedback, associated usage records, and saved third-party API credentials.

Some information may be retained when required by law or when necessary to address a specific legal or security matter. Such retention is limited to what is necessary. Copies held in service-provider backups may remain for a limited period under their respective retention practices.

Your choices

Providing your own third-party API credentials is optional. You can remove saved credentials through the app's settings. You can also request deletion of your account and associated information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the effective date when changes are made. For privacy questions or requests, contact rifter.sahil@gmail.com.
