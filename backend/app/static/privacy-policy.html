Privacy Policy

Roundtable AI
Effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how Roundtable AI handles your information when you use the app. For privacy questions, contact rifter.sahil@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account information: Your email address and, if available, your name.
  • Documents and extracted profile: job descriptions and resumes you choose to upload are used to create an interview profile. We store the extracted profile, not an archive of uploaded files.
  • Interview information: interview mode, skill focus, session timestamps, conversation text, and generated feedback.
  • Voice and device permissions: microphone audio is streamed during a live interview. We do not intentionally store raw microphone audio.
  • Waitlist information: email and name, if available, if you join the Pro waitlist.
  • Technical information: limited information about errors and app requests to help maintain and protect the service.

How information is used and shared

We use your information to provide and improve interviews, prepare your interview profile, generate feedback, manage your account and waitlist, and protect the service.

We share information with service providers that help us provide the app, including providers for account and data storage, voice interviews, and AI-powered profile and feedback features. They process information on our behalf and may handle it in other countries under their own privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users and the service, or to respond to valid legal process.

Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service. When you request account deletion, we delete your account and associated information within 48 hours. Some information may remain for a limited time in backups or where we are legally required to retain it.

If we must retain information for a legal reason, we limit it to what is necessary and delete it when it is no longer needed.

To delete your account, use the in-app Account menu or visit our account deletion page.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will be updated when we do. Contact rifter.sahil@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.
