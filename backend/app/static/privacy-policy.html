Privacy Policy

Roundtable AI
Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy describes how Roundtable AI processes information when you use the app. For privacy questions or requests, contact rifter.sahil@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account information: Your account identifier, email address, and name when available.
  • Documents and interview profile: Job descriptions and resumes you choose to submit are processed to prepare an interview profile. The backend stores the extracted profile, not an archive of the uploaded files.
  • Interview information: Your selected interview mode and skill focus, session records, conversation content used to provide the interview, and generated feedback.
  • Microphone data: Audio is transmitted during a live interview so speech can be recognized and a spoken response can be generated. We do not intentionally store raw microphone audio in our backend.
  • Waitlist information: Your email address and name, when available, if you choose to join the Pro early-access waitlist.
  • Service and diagnostic information: Information about requests and errors needed to operate, maintain, and protect the service.

How we use information

We use this information to create interview profiles, provide live interviews, generate feedback, manage accounts and waitlist requests, maintain the service, and protect it from misuse.

Service providers

Roundtable AI uses service providers to operate its features. Firebase and Google Cloud support account authentication and data storage. Agora provides live voice interview services. Groq processes document and conversation text for AI-generated profiles, interview responses, and feedback. Speech recognition and synthesis are provided through Deepgram and MiniMax services integrated with the voice experience.

Information needed for a feature may be processed by these providers on our behalf. Their processing and any international transfers are also subject to their own privacy terms.

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the service, or to respond to valid legal process.

Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide and protect the service. When you request account deletion, we delete your account and associated information within 48 hours, under normal circumstances. This includes account data, saved interview profiles and session records, feedback, quota records, and waitlist information associated with the account.

Some information may be retained when required by law or when necessary to address a specific legal or security matter. In those cases, retention is limited to what is necessary and the information is deleted when the need ends. Copies held in service provider backups may remain for a limited time under their retention practices.

To request deletion, use the Account menu in the app or follow the instructions on our account deletion page.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the effective date when we do. For privacy questions or requests, contact rifter.sahil@gmail.com.
