Delete your Roundtable AI account

You can request account deletion from the signed-in app. Open Account, select Delete account, and confirm your request. We will delete your account and associated information within 48 hours, under normal circumstances.

What will be deleted

Deletion includes your account, saved interview profiles, session records and feedback, quota records, and any Pro waitlist information associated with your account. Original job-description and resume files are not retained by the backend after processing.

If you cannot access the app

Email rifter.sahil@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and request deletion. Do not send your password. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing your request. Verified requests will be completed within 48 hours.

Retention exceptions

Information required by law or needed for a specific legal or security matter may be retained for as long as necessary to address that need. Copies held in service-provider backups may remain for a limited time under their retention practices.

For more information about how we handle information, see our Privacy Policy. For help, contact rifter.sahil@gmail.com.
